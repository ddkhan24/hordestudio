# Horde Studio 18.1.0

Virtual Humans 2.0 creation, usability and long-run simulation release.

## Rebuilt creation experience

- Organize the complete person model into a visible system map: Identity & history, Psychology & relationship, Conversation & expression, World & autonomous life, Social presence, Media, and Runtime.
- Keep complexity available without forcing it into one enormous form. Age, appearance, body/access and other core identity fields are visible; specialist controls remain reachable within their system.
- Make Chat, Edit human and Live human explicit destinations. Edit and delete are available from the human library and editor.
- Replace the detached field guide with contextual, keyboard-accessible help beside relevant fields.
- Repair editor scrolling, mobile overflow and inconsistent button/input styling.

## Safer AI drafting

- Retire whole-human generation. AI drafting is page-scoped and uses one bounded plain-text request per selected field.
- Show the exact request count before submission; opening a builder spends nothing.
- Stop a batch after a provider, rate-limit or malformed-output failure, keep completed drafts, and retry only the affected field.
- Preserve heightened, obsessive, antagonistic, eccentric and solitary character direction instead of normalizing it into an agreeable template.

## Durable editing and recovery

- Preserve long appearance and personality text with visible limits instead of silently truncating it.
- Add editable JSON template export/import alongside full portable-human backups.
- Preserve chat style, life setup and media through save/reload and archive round trips.
- Expose provider-reported token usage, redacted prompt previews, failures, and foreground/background request budgets.

## Live-human controls

- Edit and remove unused places and rooms while a life is running, with clear dependency errors for active references.
- Clarify commitments, opportunity windows and activity duration so a meal is not accidentally treated as a thirteen-hour action.
- Make pause, resume and history-preserving restart discoverable.
- Restore visible keyboard-operable gifts, mailed-gifts and money permissions.
- Separate the durable human definition from the evolving live timeline without duplicating place/room concepts.

## Deeper people

- Add mechanical mind, cognition, body/access and trigger systems while retaining freeform authorship.
- Support obsessive and unsettling archetypes, uneven cognition, adult-only triggers, mobility/sensory differences and assistive technology without reducing those traits to personality labels.
- Preserve optional player-first or human-first conversation openings.
- Repair sleep, food, travel feasibility, supporting-person meals, social-bond contact and debt repayment found by the complex 100-day scenario.

## Packaging and platform fixes

- Move Virtual Human frontend, engine and backend source into `virtual_humans/`.
- Keep the portable root to the three platform launchers, `START HERE.txt`, and the `app/` directory.
- Decode worker subprocesses as UTF-8 with replacement diagnostics, including the Windows Python 3.11 path.
- Correct platform-specific launcher guidance and keep HTTPS verification enabled with actionable CA/certificate errors.

The release gates use fixture providers and do not submit paid generation. External model quality and provider availability still vary. An expired certificate on a provider or HTTPS proxy must be renewed by its operator; Horde Studio does not disable certificate verification.

Download and extract the complete portable ZIP. Keep the `app` directory beside the launchers, then run the launcher for your operating system. Existing saves, chats and lives are preserved; keep a verified backup until you confirm the updated copy.
