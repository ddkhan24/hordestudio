# Horde Studio 18.0.0

Version 18 brings persistent Virtual Human life, shared social participants, reference-aware media and a redesigned social interface together in one application.

## Virtual Humans 2.0

- Aslyn Jonas is included as a fresh character with 28 starter posts, two private gallery photos, five clips and 62 approved visual references. All 22 authored places and six rooms have references. Personal chats, saved sessions, player relationships and memories are excluded from the bundled character.
- Persistent life shared across persona-specific conversations and relationships.
- Connected needs, geography, travel, supporting people, personal calendars, social awareness and bounded story proposals.
- Supporting people have their own spatial activity and return routes. The corrected Tempe walking pack preserves pedestrian connections and maps the Memorial Union to its authored place.
- Conversations draw on the current life and personal history, with persona-specific knowledge and optional short message bursts.
- Expanded AI builder coverage for personality, lifestyle, references, supporting people and executable life settings.
- Visible autonomy controls, background catch-up and actionable generation feedback.

## Social media and clips

- Cleaner full-height social drawer with uncropped photos, compact post headers and collapsible comments.
- Starter-profile updates preserve stable post identity and existing interactions; older duplicates are consolidated.
- On-demand clips stay outside player conversation. Public captions are separate from production instructions and can be edited.
- Private gallery ideas retain prompts and references without requiring an immediate paid image.

## Portable characters

- ZIP exports separate the manifest, images, clips and compressed life data, avoiding giant JSON strings.
- Full Portable Human retains the saved life and conversations; templates start fresh.
- Legacy JSON imports remain supported, with checksum-verified life recovery and no automatic retry of uncertain paid jobs.
- Life backups are compressed incrementally, and export failures show the actual cause.

## Updating

Extract the portable ZIP, restart Horde using the platform launcher, and refresh the browser. Keep a verified backup before replacing an installation. Python 3 and Node.js 18+ are required for the local simulation service.

Validation includes isolated multiweek life simulations, reference attachment checks, browser installation and media playback, and four bounded live conversation diagnostics. Simulation tests do not establish consciousness, universal realism or consistent output quality from every external model.
