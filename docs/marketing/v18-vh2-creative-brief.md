# Horde Studio 18 · Virtual Humans 2.0 creative brief

Prepared 13 September 2026. Production brief; nothing published. The current release calls the bundled character **Aslyn Jonas**. Use that spelling consistently, even though earlier releases featured Ashlyn “Ash” Reynolds and the request says Ashlyn.

## One idea

**The texts are a window into a life.**

Follow Aslyn across one chat, her day, her places, her people and her social profile. The audience should understand the product by seeing what she is doing and how the app records it. The emotional hook is everyday specificity: a place, a small concern, a photo, a message in her own voice. Avoid a catalogue of all Horde Studio modes.

Working Reddit title: **I built a life around AI character chat. Here’s Aslyn in Horde Studio 18.**

Opening copy: “The part I wanted to build was everything around the conversation: where a character is, what their day looks like, who else is in it, and what they share. Virtual Humans 2.0 connects that simulated life to their conversations. Here’s a look through Aslyn’s chat, map and social profile in the actual app.”

## Seven-slide storyboard

Use a consistent 1080 × 1350 canvas. Keep 64 px outer margins. A small `Horde Studio 18 / Virtual Humans 2.0` identifier, one 5–10-word headline and one short explanation are enough. The genuine app view should occupy roughly three quarters of most slides. Warm off-white type, charcoal background and restrained coral retain the brand; media supplies the colour. No invented messages, captions, photos, activity records or UI.

| # | Headline | Actual evidence to capture | Layout and meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The texts are a window into a life.** | Aslyn’s header and a short actual exchange with a concrete reference to her day, location or shared history. | Tight chat crop plus a small real portrait/photo. Readable bubbles are the hero. Caption: “Meet Aslyn in Virtual Humans 2.0.” Use only a shareable conversation; if it is a newly created demo, label it. |
| 2 | **See where her day takes her.** | Map with Aslyn’s recorded location and, when present, an actual journey or destination. ## Grounded claims and practical copy

Sources: `docs/releases/v18.0.0.md` and `docs/vh2/START-HERE.md`.

- Aslyn is bundled with 28 starter posts, two private gallery photos, five clips and 62 approved visual references. All 22 authored places and six rooms have references. These are package contents, not a count of new autonomous events.
- Multiple player personas have distinct conversations and relationships within one persistent life. Show this only if a clean demo demonstrates it; do not expose personal player history.
- Conversations use current life and personal history; short message bursts are optional. No guarantee of naturalness for every model.
- Background life requires the local server to remain running. Catch-up resumes after downtime; the computer does not simulate while off. Avoid unqualified “always alive” or “keeps living when your computer is off.”
- Provider keys are configured in Settings → Connections. Cloud generation and some data require configuration. Provider charges are real; the simulated money is fictional. No “free unlimited photos/video.”
- The simulator models continuity, needs and decisions. No consciousness, sentience, human-equivalence or universal realism claims. Do not use the previous “A person, not a prompt” line.

Post body should identify the author as the developer, explain that this is a local app with a simulation service, give the verified v18 release/source links, and state Python 3 + Node.js 18+ and provider requirements. Verify the actual license before choosing “open source” versus “source available.” Closing question: **Which part of a character’s life would you want to see reflected in the next conversation?**

## Production acceptance

Capture current v18 screens with one consistent Aslyn identity. Record which images are bundled starters, which are existing saved-life media and which are newly produced demo output. Do not rearrange separate events into a false causal story. Read every visible bubble and caption for personal details before using it. Preserve the actual capture as source; make crops and explanatory annotations on the final composition only. At a 360 px-wide preview, the key exchange, caption and primary UI labels must remain readable. Check for cut-off photos, accidental modal overlays, empty states and mismatched timestamps. Review every exported slide and the playable clip before delivery. Publishing is outside this brief.
